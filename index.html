Local task data and offline tools

Task titles, next-step notes, schedules, repeating templates, focus history, appearance settings and advertising choices are stored in local app storage. Core tasks, timers, notes, statistics and backups work offline. We do not provide accounts or cloud synchronization, and do not send your task or note contents to the advertising service.

Optional InMobi advertising and consent management

The app includes InMobi advertising and its consent-management platform (CMP). These services are optional. Before requesting them, the app asks whether you are 18 or older and whether you want to review advertising choices. Undecided, off and under-18 choices do not start advertising or CMP requests. Every core tool remains available.

Adults who choose advertising can review the CMP choices applicable to their region. The app requests ads only after it can verify the applicable consent and opt-out signals. Unknown, incomplete, failed or opted-out states do not enable ads. The app does not invent a TC string or treat silence as consent.

When these services run, InMobi may process IP address and approximate region derived from it, advertising identifier, device and operating-system information, app version, language, network information, consent records, ad impressions, clicks and advertising diagnostics. Uses include advertising delivery and measurement, security, fraud prevention and consent management. The app does not request precise or coarse Android location permission; its publisher configuration blocks app-collected location access.

Read InMobi’s privacy policy for its processing, partners, retention, international transfers and rights channels.

Your choices

Settings → Advertising & privacy lets you keep advertising off or change your coarse age choice. Adults who opted in can open Manage advertising consent to review the CMP. Disabling ads stops new ad requests and removes the ad view; a request already started may finish. Reopening the app with advertising off prevents advertising SDK initialization. No birth date is requested. Where applicable, the CMP provides choices about targeted advertising and sale or sharing of data; verified opt-out signals prevent ad requests. You can also reset or delete the advertising identifier in Android settings.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access support optional advertising, consent management and policy links. Advertising-ID permission supports eligible adult advertising after consent checks. Notifications, Alarms & reminders, and boot notifications support timers. Backup import and export use the Android file picker. The app does not request contacts, microphone, camera, accessibility or broad storage access.

Backups, retention and deletion

Android automatic cloud backup and automatic app-data transfer are excluded. You may export a plain-text JSON file to a location you choose. A cloud file provider you select handles that file under its own policy. Backups include tasks, templates, generation records and saved focus history, but exclude the running timer, advertising consent and appearance settings. Keep exported notes in a trusted location.

Local data remains until you delete it, restore a replacement backup, clear app storage or uninstall. Deleting a task retains saved focus history, including the recorded task name. To remove all local records use Android Settings → Apps → FocusNext → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall. Remove exported files separately. InMobi’s retention and deletion follow its own policy and rights channels.

Younger users

FocusNext supports study, work and everyday planning. Users under 18 can use the offline core functions. The under-18 choice does not initialize advertising or CMP services. We do not request a child’s name, birth date, account or parental contact. Contact us if you believe data was submitted incorrectly.

Contact and changes

Questions about FocusNext or local data can be sent to kedgeyuan@gmail.com. Feature or data-practice changes will be reflected in this policy, its date and in-app disclosures.

本机数据与离线工具

任务名称、下一步笔记、日期、重复模板、专注记录、外观偏好和广告选择保存在应用本机存储中。任务、计时、笔记、统计和备份等核心功能离线可用。我们不提供账号或云同步服务，不向广告服务发送任务名称或笔记内容。

可选广告与同意管理

应用包含 InMobi 广告和同意管理平台（CMP）。启动前会询问是否已满18岁以及是否查看广告隐私选项。尚未选择、保持关闭广告、选择未满18岁时，不启动广告或CMP请求，全部核心工具仍然可用。

已满18岁且选择广告后，CMP联网展示适用地区的选项。应用只有核实相关同意与退出信号后才请求广告；未知、未完成、错误或已退出状态不启用广告，不伪造TC字符串，不将未回答视为同意。

服务运行时，InMobi可能处理IP地址及据此推断的大致地区、广告标识符、设备和系统信息、应用版本、语言、网络信息、同意记录、广告展示与点击、广告诊断，用于广告投放与衡量、安全、反作弊和同意管理。应用不申请Android精确或粗略定位权限；发布者配置禁止访问应用收集的定位数据。其合作方、保留、跨境传输和权利渠道见InMobi隐私政策。

更改选择

可在“设置→广告与隐私”关闭广告或更改粗略年龄选择。已选择广告的成年人可在“管理广告同意选项”重新查看CMP。关闭后停止新的广告请求并移除广告视图，已开始的请求可能完成；关闭广告后重新打开应用，不初始化广告SDK。不要求填写出生日期。在适用地区，CMP提供定向广告、数据出售或共享等退出选项；核实退出信号时不请求广告。也可在Android设置中重置或删除广告标识符。

权限

联网与网络状态权限用于可选广告、CMP及政策链接；广告标识符权限用于符合年龄和同意条件的成人广告。通知、闹钟提醒和开机通知用于计时；备份通过系统文件选择器读写。不申请通讯录、麦克风、相机、无障碍或广泛存储权限。

备份、保留和删除

应用关闭Android自动云备份并排除自动应用数据迁移。可主动导出明文JSON备份到自行选择的位置；选择云盘文件提供程序时，该服务按自己的政策处理文件。备份包含任务、模板、生成记录及已保存专注历史，不包含运行中计时、广告同意和外观设置。请将笔记备份放在可信位置。

本机数据保留至主动删除、恢复替换备份、清除应用存储或卸载。删除任务保留此前记录的专注历史及当时保存的任务名称。删除全部记录可在Android“设置→应用→FocusNext→存储→清除存储”操作或卸载；导出文件需单独删除。InMobi持有数据的保留和删除遵循其政策，可通过其权利渠道提出请求。

未成年人

应用用于学习、工作和日常任务规划。未满18岁的用户可使用离线核心工具；选择未满18岁时不初始化广告或CMP。不要求儿童提供姓名、出生日期、账号或家长联系方式。如认为数据被错误提交，请联系我们。

联系与更新

关于FocusNext或本机数据的问题，请联系kedgeyuan@gmail.com。功能或处理方式改变时会更新本政策、生效日期和应用内说明。
